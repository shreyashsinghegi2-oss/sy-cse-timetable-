---
name: Microtask source permissions
description: Why commonly suggested freelance APIs cannot automatically become StudentLancing Micro Tasks sources.
---

Treat Micro Tasks as short, paid freelance projects across subjects, not full-time/contract job postings relabeled as tasks. Keep genuine in-app postings available; do not substitute fabricated external records or discovery links.

Current product decision: Until a suitable, permitted external source is established, Micro Tasks are paused in the student-facing app and new-posting controls. Preserve existing task postings, applications, and employer/admin access to historical records so the feature can be restored later.

**Why:** The user chose to remove Micro Tasks for now rather than use an unsuitable feed. Pausing the feature should not erase agreements or application history.

**How to apply:** Restore browsing or posting only after a source and product scope have been approved; retain past application tracking while paused.

**Why:** Freelancer.com's official API terms prohibit using its API to replicate or compete with Freelancer's services. Algora's terms restrict commercial reuse of its service content and automated access. Taskmarket's documented public API covers real paid tasks, but its marketplace is strongly agent- and USDC-wallet-focused, and its posted legal terms were marked as an inactive counsel draft when reviewed. These are not established permissions and fit for a general student task feed.

**How to apply:** Before enabling another external provider, verify explicit API/feed access and reuse rights, human eligibility, payment model, live open/deadline evidence, original task links, and attribution. Do not assume that a public endpoint or an open-source website license grants rights over its users' listings.
