name: Career Compass track meaning
description: Business interpretation of the two Career Map tracks
---

Treat Personal and Institutional Career Maps as two paths within the same Career Compass system. Their student-facing steps, roadmap interface, and progress controls should match; only outward wording and partner responsibilities differ. The institutional path represents StudentXchange's agreement/knowledge-partner relationship with an institution, not a separate product. Preserve institution-published roadmaps and the existing ready institutions.

**Why:** The user clarified that a student entering either route should receive the same inner experience, while institution-published content and institutional responsibilities remain intact.

**How to apply:** When describing or changing Career Compass, frame the shared system first, keep interface parity between the two routes, and distinguish delivery and responsibility by track. Do not remove published institutional content or partner institutions.
